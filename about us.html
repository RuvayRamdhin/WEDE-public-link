About us

Established in 1986, Sportsmans Warehouse has grown into Southern Africa's premier destination for sporting equipment, athletic apparel, and technical services. Operating major-format superstores nationwide, our mission is to equip athletes of every level from beginners to seasoned competitors with high-performance gear, trusted advice, and dedicated in-store support.

Our Mission & Vision

We are dedicated to fostering active lifestyles across South Africa by offering top global brands, expert equipment preparation, and reliable customer service across all major sports disciplines.
